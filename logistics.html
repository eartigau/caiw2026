Organizing Committee

The workshop is organized by a team of Canadian astronomers committed to growing and strengthening the instrumentation community. The organizing committee is being finalized and will be announced shortly.

We welcome your involvement. If you have suggestions for discussion topics, would like to help shape the agenda, or are interested in participating in the organizing committee, please reach out. We look forward to seeing the community together in Montréal!
